## METERS SECTION
Heading:    SIX NUMBERS. ONE FACILITY.
Subheading: Hover a meter to see what it controls.

WATER USE / L per kWh
How much water the facility burns per unit of compute.
Let this climb and the creek pays the price.

POWER DRAW / kW
Total electrical load across all systems.
Cut power wrong and you lose the whole stack.

HEAT INDEX / °C delta
Temperature difference between intake and discharge.
Above threshold: regulatory breach. Above critical: system failure.

CREEK HEALTH / % baseline
What the water downstream looks like right now.
This is the number the regulator checks first.

COMMUNITY TRUST / 0–100
What the neighbourhood outside the fence knows.
Once this hits zero, no decision matters anymore.

UPTIME / %
The facility's core obligation.
Everything else is negotiable. This one almost isn't.
